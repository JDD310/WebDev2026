Champions of LoL Esports World Championship

This is a list of all the champions from the League of Legends World Championship.

List of Champions

  1. 2011 - Fnatic

    2. Fnatic, a European team, won the inaugural League of Legends World Championship in 2011. Their victory set the stage for the competitive scene and established them as one of the top teams in the world.

  2. 2012 - Taipei Assassins

    3. Taipei Assassins, a Taiwanese team, reached the finals in 2012 but were defeated by Fnatic. Their performance marked a significant moment in the competitive scene.

  3. 2013 - SK Telecom T1

    4. SK Telecom T1, a South Korean team, won their first World Championship in 2013. This victory marked the beginning of their dominance in the competitive scene, as they went on to win multiple championships in the following years.

  4. 2014 - Samsung White

    5. Samsung White, a South Korean team, won the 2014 World Championship. Their victory was a testament to their skill and strategic gameplay.

  5. 2015 - SK Telecom T1

    6. SK Telecom T1 won their second World Championship in 2015. Their consistency and excellence made them one of the most successful teams in the history of League of Legends esports.

  6. 2016 - SK Telecom T1

    7. SK Telecom T1 won their third World Championship in 2016. Their dominance in the competitive scene continued, solidifying their legacy as one of the greatest teams in League of Legends history.

  7. 2017 - Samsung Galaxy

    8. Samsung Galaxy won the 2017 World Championship. Their victory was a significant moment in the competitive scene, showcasing their skill and teamwork.

  8. 2018 - Invictus Gaming

    9. Invictus Gaming, a Chinese team, won the 2018 World Championship. Their victory was a significant milestone for the region and showcased the growing strength of Chinese teams in the global esports scene.

  9. 2019 - FunPlus Phoenix

    10. FunPlus Phoenix, a Chinese team, reached the finals in 2019 but were defeated by Invictus Gaming. Their performance marked a significant moment in the competitive scene.

  10. 2020 - DAMWON Gaming

    11. DAMWON Gaming, a South Korean team, won the 2020 World Championship. Their strategic gameplay and exceptional teamwork were key factors in their success.

  11. 2021 - EDward Gaming

    12. EDward Gaming, a Chinese team, reached the finals in 2021 but were defeated by DAMWON Gaming. Their performance marked a significant moment in the competitive scene.

  12. 2022 - DRX

    13. In 2022, DRX, another South Korean team, made headlines by winning the World Championship. Their journey to victory was marked by resilience and determination, as they overcame numerous challenges to claim the title.

  13. 2023 - T1

    14. T1 continued their legacy by winning the World Championship in 2023. Their consistent performance and strategic prowess have made them one of the most successful teams in the history of League of Legends esports. This victory was also the beginning of a new era for the team.

  14. 2024 - T1

    15. T1 earned another victory in 2024 after a year of intense competition. Their ability to adapt and innovate in their strategies allowed them to maintain their position at the top of the competitive scene.

  15. 2025 - T1

    16. T1 won the 2025 World Championship, securing a 3-Peat, a feat which will likely never be matched.
